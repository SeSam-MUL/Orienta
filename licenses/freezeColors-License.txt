Which grant covers our copy
---------------------------
The revision we ship is v2.3, whose last changelog entry is dated April 2010.
It carries the author's own grant in its header, and that grant is the one our
copy is distributed under:

The upstream project added a formal MIT licence on 2024-06-30, long after this
revision and alongside version 2.5. Its text is reproduced below because it is
the author's own, later statement of the same permission, and because a reader
checking our attribution against upstream will find MIT there. It is included
for reference; it is not a claim that the 2024 text was attached to v2.3.
