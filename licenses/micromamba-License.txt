On macOS the Orienta installer downloads the micromamba binary (mamba-org,
https://github.com/mamba-org/mamba; releases at
https://github.com/mamba-org/micromamba-releases, version and SHA-256 pinned in
electron/platform.js) onto the user's machine, runs it to build the Python
environment from conda-forge, and keeps it in Orienta's runtime folder. Orienta
does not redistribute micromamba. Its license is reproduced below because the

Statically linked components of the micromamba binary
------------------------------------------------------

The released binary is extracted from the conda-forge build of micromamba
(the release asset is the bare executable). The conda-forge recipe declares
the combined license "BSD-3-Clause AND MIT AND OpenSSL" and ships, in the
conda package's `info/licenses/`, the license texts of these linked libraries:
CLI11, libcurl, c-ares, fmt, krb5, libarchive, libev, LZ4, libnghttp2,
OpenSSL, libsolv, nlohmann-json, reproc, spdlog, tl-expected, zlib and zstd.
Further static host dependencies of that build (simdjson, yaml-cpp, libpsl,
libssh2, xz, libmsgpack-c) are not in that list; their texts stay with their
own upstreams. The recipe is at
https://github.com/conda-forge/micromamba-feedstock, the package on the
conda-forge channel; that is where a user who passes the binary on will find
the texts. Orienta neither modifies nor redistributes the binary.
